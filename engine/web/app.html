Your job search, on your own computer

Job Agent finds postings that fit you. You decide on each one, it drafts the documents, and you apply yourself.

How it works

  1. Job sitesthe countries, sites and employers you chose
  2. Searchnow, or every morning by itself
  3. Matchesjobs that fit your words and places; 0 to decide
  4. You decideAccept or Reject each one
  5. DraftsCV and cover letter, on your computer
  6. You applyyourself, when you are ready
Optional: AI drafts with your own key, only when you click Optional: a daily online search on your own Cloudflare account

Safe by design

Nothing is sent without you

Job Agent never applies, submits or e-mails anything. Every posting waits for your Accept or Reject, and documents are drafts you open, edit and send yourself.

Your personal data stays with you

Your CV, profile, decisions and documents are files in your own folder on this computer. They leave it only if you use AI with your own key, and then only for that request.

This online page holds only postings, your decisions and search settings. Your CV, profile and documents stay on your computer.

What goes over the internet

Your search words and places go to the job sites you chose; job pages are read by their address; AI only when you click it. No tracking or analytics. See every request

AI help (optional)

With your own Anthropic API key, the app can read your CV into your profile, draft tailored CVs and cover letters, and review new jobs. This sends that material to Anthropic's API when you use it. Without a key, use "Copy for Claude" and any Claude chat instead.

Optional search services

Online search (optional)

If you deployed Job Agent on your own Cloudflare account, the search runs there every day and this app fetches the jobs and your decisions from it. Your CV and documents stay here.

Network activity

What this app received from the internet and what it sent, recorded on this computer for every request, including the ones made in the background (daily run, sync). API keys are never shown or recorded.

What can leave this computer, and when
  • Your search words and places go to the job sources you switched on (Setup > Countries and job sites), during a search.
  • Employer job boards, careers pages and posting pages are only requested by address; nothing about you is sent.
  • To Anthropic, with your own key only: when you press "Write with AI" or read a CV with AI, and after each search if "Review new jobs with AI" is on. Each entry below names the material it carried.
  • To your online Job Agent, if connected: your accept/reject decisions, which jobs have documents, and your employer list. Never your CV, profile or documents.
  • To GitHub, when you check for or install an update: nothing about you.
  • Never: no analytics, tracking or reports to anyone else.
  • Not in this list: installing the Python packages on first start (from pypi.org), and the extra files a page loads itself when it is read in the headless browser.
