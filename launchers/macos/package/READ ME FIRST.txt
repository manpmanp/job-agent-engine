Job Agent for macOS
===================

1. Drag "Job Agent" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click "Job Agent".
   The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app for malicious
   software (the app is not signed by Apple yet):
   - click Done (or Cancel),
   - open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to "Job Agent", then confirm.
   This is needed only once.
3. Job Agent opens in your web browser. It runs only on your Mac.
   Your job search is kept in the "JobAgent" folder in your home folder.

To stop it: close the Job Agent tab (it stops by itself a few seconds later),
or click Quit the app at the top of the page.
To open it again: double-click "Job Agent" (if it is already running,
your browser is simply pointed at it).

Update:     double-click "Update Job Agent" in this window (or in the app:
            Settings > Update and uninstall > Check for updates).
Uninstall:  double-click "Uninstall Job Agent" in this window (or in the
            app: Settings > Update and uninstall > Uninstall). It removes
            the app, its daily run and settings; your JobAgent folder only
            if you tick it.

Help and source code: https://github.com/manpmanp/job-agent-engine
