Job Agent for Linux
===================

1. Unpack:      tar xzf JobAgent-Linux.tar.gz
2. Install:     cd JobAgent-Linux && ./install.sh
   (copies Job Agent to ~/.local/bin and adds it to your applications
   menu; no administrator rights needed)
3. Start it from the applications menu ("Job Agent"), or run: job-agent

Job Agent opens in your web browser and runs only on your computer.
Your job search is kept in ~/JobAgent.
To stop it: Settings > Quit the app.
Uninstall:  ./install.sh --remove   (your ~/JobAgent folder is kept)

Help and source code: https://github.com/manpmanp/job-agent-engine
