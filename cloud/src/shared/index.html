Connect your computer

CVs, cover letters and documents never come here. To make documents for the postings you accept, open the Job Agent app on your computer, go to Settings > Online search, and enter this page's address and the sync token you chose when you deployed it. The app then fetches your postings and decisions and makes the documents on your computer.

Address:
Online search (optional)

If you deployed Job Agent on your own Cloudflare account, the search runs there every day and this app fetches the postings and your decisions from it. Your CV and documents stay here.

Run every morning
Searches on your search day and refreshes on the other days, even when this window is closed. Your computer must be on (a Mac catches up after waking).
AI help (optional)

With your own Anthropic API key, the app can read your CV into your profile, draft tailored CVs and cover letters, and review new postings. This sends that material to Anthropic's API when you use it. Without a key, use "Copy for Claude" and any Claude chat instead.
