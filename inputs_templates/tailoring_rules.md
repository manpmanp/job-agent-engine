# Personal tailoring rules (OPTIONAL)

Rules that apply only to you and that the agent must follow on top of the
generic rules in `skills/tailor-documents/SKILL.md` when it writes your CV
and cover letters. Leave this file empty if you have none.

Examples of what belongs here:

- An entry that must appear in every CV (for example your current position,
  with exact wording).
- Projects that must never be merged into one bullet, and the exact wording
  to use for each.
- Which of your source documents is the authoritative one for a given kind
  of fact (for example a publication list you maintain yourself).
- Style corrections you have given before and do not want to repeat.

Rules here never permit inventing experience. Anything the agent writes
must be traceable to your profile, your uploaded documents or your
reference files.
