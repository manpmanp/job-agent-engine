Start from a CV or cover letter you already have

A browser page cannot place files in your agent home, so prepared documents go in with the runner. The original is kept as the wording reference for tailoring, and its text is extracted for the agent to turn into a profile.

./run.sh intake cv  path/to/my_cv.pdf
./run.sh intake cl  path/to/a_letter_I_sent.docx

Supported: .docx, .pdf, .txt, .md. You can combine both routes: upload a CV now, add form entries later. When a newer entry differs from an older one from the other route, the newest is used and the change is listed for you to confirm:

./run.sh intake conflicts
./run.sh intake resolve 1 keep-previous
